# Physical paint-testing guide

## Do not start with the gallon

1. Stir Vining Ivy thoroughly. Label three clean, sealable cups with candidate, date, and ratio.
2. Measure **8.00 fl oz of Vining Ivy** into each cup. Use graduated syringes for sub-ounce additions; 1 fl oz = 2 tablespoons = 6 teaspoons.
3. Test these first (descriptive lab names, not PPG colors or formulas):
   - **Warm Sage:** 8 oz Vining Ivy + **0.63 oz warm greige** (about 1 tbsp + 0.8 tsp).
   - **Muted Moss:** 8 oz Vining Ivy + **0.50 oz neutral gray** (1 tbsp).
   - **Soft Eucalyptus:** 8 oz Vining Ivy + **1.00 oz soft warm white** (2 tbsp).
4. Use only an additive compatible in chemistry, product family, sheen, and base. If uncertain, ask a paint professional and do a tiny compatibility test first. Never assume a universal colorant can be safely dosed by volume.
5. Mix consistently, make two coats on movable primed sample boards, and note the exact measurements. Let each coat dry per the product label.
6. Move boards beside Sharkskin and Saw Mill. Observe morning/day/evening and the actual 2700–5000K fixtures for at least 48 hours.
7. Check adhesion, texture, cure, rub resistance, and unwanted metamerism. Reject any curdling, separation, gloss mismatch, or slow cure.
8. Iterate in tiny increments. Only scale the winning *measured ratio* after a full dry-down. Leave headroom in the gallon and retain an untouched control sample.

The app's HEX swatches and ounce ratios are directional experiments—not official PPG tint formulas or performance guidance.
